ACT 01 — SUBMIT

Orders enter as ciphertext

Each trader sends an encrypted amount into the batch contract. On-chain, observers see that someone submitted — never how much. Every value is a 32-byte handle; the plaintext never touches the chain.

Vision & the problem it solves

Public blockchains made finance auditable — and, as a side effect, exposed. On a transparent AMM, order size, wallet, and timing are all legible to anyone, which invites front-running and copy-trading and quietly blocks serious capital from ever arriving.

Umbra closes that gap without asking the ecosystem to migrate. It sits as a confidential layer in front of Uniswap: traders submit encrypted orders, Umbra batches and nets them inside a Trusted Execution Environment, and only the aggregate reaches the public pool. Individual size is never revealed, yet the swap settles on the same deep, composable liquidity everyone already uses.

How it works

Umbra is built on iExec Nox — a privacy layer where confidential values live as handles and computation runs inside Intel TDX enclaves. The chain only ever stores encrypted references; the aggregate swap is the single point that touches daylight.

01

Submit

Traders hold cUSDC (a confidential wrapped ERC-20) and send an encrypted amount to the batch contract during an open window.

02

Aggregate

The TEE sums the encrypted orders and unwraps only the total — revealing the batch size, never any individual's.

03

Settle

One aggregate swap executes on real Uniswap on Ethereum Sepolia. Uniswap is untouched; composability is intact.

04

Redistribute

Output is credited pro-rata as encrypted cWETH balances, using the public post-swap rate as a scalar multiplier.

05

Disclose (optional)

Any trader can grant a scoped, revocable ACL so an auditor reads their amount alone — selective, on-chain, reversible.

How to use it

  1. ConnectAny standard Ethereum wallet — no special client, no migration. Switch to Ethereum Sepolia.
  2. WrapTurn public USDC into confidential cUSDC. Your balance becomes a hidden, encrypted handle.
  3. Submit an orderChoose an amount to swap for WETH. Umbra encrypts it and drops it into the current batch — invisible in the mempool.
  4. Wait for the batchWhen the window closes, Umbra executes one aggregate swap on Uniswap and credits your private share.
  5. Read or revealView your shielded cWETH balance, unwrap when you like, or grant an auditor selective access.
